The Myth
In Japanese folklore, the kitsune grows a new tail
for every century of wisdom earned —
and nine tails means she is something
the world has never fully tamed.

Yasmin the Fox lives between two worlds. On the surface: golden light, ocean air, loose linen and bare feet on warm sand. The girl you'd follow without quite knowing why you can't look away.

Underneath: something older. Her stories don't end where you expect them to. They stop just before the climax — and the rest lives somewhere darker, somewhere you have to earn your way into.
